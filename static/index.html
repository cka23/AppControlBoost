About AppControlUI beta

AppControlUI helps you discover the applications your SentinelOne agents report and plan Application Control rules from them. Because there is no App Control API, it produces a CSV for you to review and upload to the console yourself. It never changes your console.

Free, best-effort, no warranty

This tool is provided free of charge, as-is, on a best-effort basis, with no warranty or support commitment of any kind. It is not an official SentinelOne product. Use it at your own risk.

Application names, publishers, versions, hashes, installation folders and the built-in Common applications list may be incomplete, out of date or inaccurate. Hashes can differ by version, and internet look-ups (winget, Homebrew) can be wrong or unavailable. Always review every rule before uploading it - especially Block rules and publisher-wide rules - and test in a limited scope first. Your data

Your API token stays in this browser tab and is only passed through this server to your console. Rules, exports and "learned" applications are saved in the server's data folder; they may contain names, paths and hashes from your environment, so keep that folder out of source control and share it carefully.
